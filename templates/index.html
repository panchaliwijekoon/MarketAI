HOW MARKETAI WORKS

From international data to an explainable recommendation

The system combines machine learning with multi-criteria decision analysis.

01

Market Data

Historical furniture import data is prepared from UN Comtrade together with external economic indicators.

02

AI Prediction

A Linear Regression model analyses historical market behaviour and forecasts 2026 import demand.

03

WSM Analysis

Predicted demand is combined with growth, GDP, logistics, political stability and tariff accessibility.

04

Recommendation

Markets are ranked according to the SME's priorities and presented with an explainable suitability score.

HYBRID DECISION SUPPORT

AI predicts. MarketAI explains.

The machine-learning component estimates future market demand. The Weighted Sum Model then combines the prediction with business and market-access indicators.

This separation keeps the final recommendation transparent rather than allowing the AI forecast alone to choose a destination.
