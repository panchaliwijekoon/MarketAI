SYSTEM METHODOLOGY

How MarketAI Generates Recommendations

MarketAI combines historical international trade data, machine-learning forecasting and a Weighted Sum Model to support export-market selection for Sri Lankan SMEs.

01

Historical Data

UN Comtrade furniture import observations for HS 940360 from 2021–2025 are collected and prepared.

→
02

AI Forecasting

Linear Regression learns historical relationships and forecasts future market demand.

→
03

Market Indicators

GDP, logistics, political stability and tariff information are added.

→
04

WSM Ranking

All indicators are normalized, weighted and combined into a market suitability score.

DATA SOURCES

Market Intelligence Inputs

UN Comtrade

Historical import-demand data for wooden furniture under HS code 940360.

Period: 2021–2025

World Bank

GDP per capita, Logistics Performance Index and political-stability indicators.

Latest available observations used

Tariff Data

Product-specific applied tariff rates for Sri Lankan exports of HS 940360 furniture.

Lower tariff = better accessibility

MACHINE LEARNING

Linear Regression Forecasting

Prediction Formula

ŷ = β₀ + β₁x₁ + β₂x₂ + β₃x₃

The regression coefficients are learned automatically from historical observations.

Model Inputs

  • Previous-year import demand
  • Current import demand
  • Historical growth percentage

The target value is the next year's import demand.

DATA PREPROCESSING

Logarithmic Transformation

x′ = ln(1 + x)

International trade values vary from relatively small markets to markets worth billions of dollars. Log transformation reduces the influence of extremely large observations during model training.

MODEL EVALUATION

Prediction Performance

R² 0.9713

Measures how much variation in the held-out observations is explained by the model.

MAE $45.24M

Mean Absolute Error represents the average absolute difference between actual and predicted values.

RMSE $128.52M

Root Mean Squared Error gives greater emphasis to larger prediction errors.

DECISION MODEL

Weighted Sum Model

Final Ranking Formula

Sᵢ = Σ wⱼ rᵢⱼ

Each normalized market indicator is multiplied by its importance weight. The weighted values are then summed to create the market score.

Six Ranking Criteria

  • AI-predicted demand
  • AI-predicted growth
  • GDP per capita
  • Logistics performance
  • Political stability
  • Applied tariff

NORMALISATION

Making Different Indicators Comparable

Benefit Criteria

Higher values are preferred.

r = (x − min) / (max − min)

Used for demand, growth, GDP, logistics and political stability.

Cost Criteria

Lower values are preferred.

r = (max − x) / (max − min)

Used for applied tariff.

PROTOTYPE LIMITATIONS

Interpretation of Results

MarketAI is a decision-support prototype rather than a guarantee of future commercial performance. Machine-learning forecasts are estimates based on historical relationships.

Different supporting indicators may use different latest available years because datasets such as the Logistics Performance Index are not published annually.

The current final WSM ranking is restricted to markets for which suitable product-specific tariff information was available.
